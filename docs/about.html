About The Sporting Almanac

The Sporting Almanac is a free, searchable archive of elite sport — built to preserve the results, seasons, players and champions that make up sporting history, all in one place.

What you'll find here

Season-by-season results, match details, player careers and records across a wide range of sports, including:

  • Australian sport — AFL, NRL and rugby league, the A-League, and the Big Bash League
  • Horse racing — Group and Listed race results in Australia and around the world, with jockey and trainer records
  • Football — the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, the Champions League and more
  • Cricket — Test cricket, World Cups, the IPL and T20 competitions
  • US sports — the NFL, NBA, NHL and Major League Baseball
  • Motorsport, combat and individual sports — Formula 1, NASCAR, Supercars, Bathurst, UFC, tennis, golf, cycling and the Olympics

Why it exists

Sporting history is scattered across thousands of pages, books and record lists. The Sporting Almanac brings it together in a consistent, easy-to-browse format, so you can look up a player's full career, compare teams head to head, or relive a season in a few clicks.

Our horse racing archive is compiled and maintained by hand, covering Group and Listed results going back to the 1800s.

Accuracy

We work hard to keep every record accurate, but with this much history, mistakes can slip through. If you spot something wrong or missing, please let us know and we'll fix it.
