HONG KONG INTERNATIONAL RACES

Every December, the global racing world turns its attention to Hong Kong, as the sport’s elite converge for one of the most prestigious days in world racing. Featuring international Group races, championship-winning horses, and the world’s leading jockeys and trainers, the Hong Kong International Races stand as a true global showcase of excellence.
