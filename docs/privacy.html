Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Overview

The Sporting Almanac ("we", "us") runs thesportingalmanac.com. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the site and how it is used. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell personal information.

Information collected

  • Usage information. Like most websites, we collect general information about how the site is used — for example pages viewed, time on site, approximate location (country or city), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. This is collected through Google Analytics and does not identify you by name.
  • Emails you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use it only to respond to you.
  • Your browser's local storage. Some pages may remember simple preferences (such as a filter you selected) in your own browser. This stays on your device.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics, which uses cookies to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. Google processes this information according to its own privacy policy. You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

We may show advertising on the site, including ads served by Google and its partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

Hosting and service providers

The site is hosted and delivered using third-party services (including GitHub Pages and Cloudflare), which may process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver pages securely and reliably.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, although some preferences may not be remembered.
  • You can contact us to ask about, correct or delete any personal information we hold about you (for example, emails you have sent us).

Children

The site is a general-interest sports archive and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@thesportingalmanac.com.
