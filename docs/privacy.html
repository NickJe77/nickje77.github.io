Privacy

What we keep about you, why, and what we don't do with it. Last updated 28 November 2026.

The Sporting Almanac form guide is free to use without an account. An account only adds a few extras: printing racebooks, keeping your Blackbook on every device, and email alerts when your horses or races come up.

What we keep when you create an account

  • Your email address, so you can sign in, reset your password, and get the alerts you've asked for.
  • Your password, scrambled. We store a one-way hash of it, never the password itself. Nobody here can read it, which is why a forgotten password is reset by email rather than sent to you.
  • When you joined and last signed in.
  • Your Blackbook: the horses and races you save, any notes you add to them, and whether you've switched email alerts on.

What stays in your own browser

  • Your sign-in, so you don't have to log in on every visit.
  • Your My Price figures, and your Blackbook if you're not signed in. These never leave your device.

What we use it for

  • Signing you in and keeping your account secure.
  • Sending the emails you ask for: password resets, and Blackbook alerts if you switch them on. Every alert email has a link to turn alerts off.
  • Simple visit counts (which pages are used most), so we know what to improve.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your email address or anything else about you.
  • We don't send marketing or pass your details to bookmakers or advertisers.
  • We don't show ads that track you.

Who helps us run it

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database where your account is kept.
  • Resend delivers our emails. It sees your address only to send the message.

Your choices

  • Turn email alerts off at any time on your Blackbook page, or with the link in any alert.
  • To see what we hold about you, correct it, or have your account deleted, reply to any email we've sent you, or contact us through The Sporting Almanac. We'll delete your account and everything saved with it.
